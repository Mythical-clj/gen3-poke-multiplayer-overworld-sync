Game

Before pressing anything: cartridge slot empty, cable plugged in, then turn the GBA on. After the load, insert the cartridge while it is running.

Tools

Live map ↗ Download the full log

The fake partner plays your friend so you can test the Cable Club on your own: start it together with the game, then walk your GBA to the trade attendant. Success = «LINKUP COMPLETE» in the log and the GBA walking into the room.

Settings

These must match between you and your friend: the same room and the same relay. Whoever hosts opens the port on their router.

Who you are in this game

Real GBAs, mGBA emulators and browsers play together: all you need is the same relay, the same room and different peer numbers. For the emulator use the emulator package (or net\run-local.ps1).

Room chat

Read-only: what your friends type on the website shows up here too. To reply, open the website and press «Just watch» (it does not take a spot in the room).
